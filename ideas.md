### <idea>
**Design Movement**: **Neo-Brutalism meets Data Futurism**

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Raw Honesty**: Expose the grid, use high-contrast borders, and avoid hiding the structure. The market is about raw data and truth.
2.  **Data-First Hierarchy**: Numbers (probabilities, prices) are the heroes. They should be large, bold, and monospaced.
3.  **High-Voltage Energy**: The design should feel electric and urgent, reflecting the real-time nature of prediction markets.
4.  **Tactile Interactivity**: Buttons and cards should have hard shadows and visible "press" states, mimicking physical trading desks.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Background**: Stark White (#FFFFFF) or extremely light grey for maximum contrast.
*   **Primary**: Electric Blue (#0055FF) for trust and technology, but saturated to feel digital-native.
*   **Secondary**: Acid Green (#CCFF00) for profit/positive outcomes and Hot Pink (#FF00CC) for risk/negative outcomes.
*   **Text**: Jet Black (#000000) for uncompromising readability.
*   **Intent**: To convey that this is a tool for serious players who want clarity and speed, not soft comfort.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Modular Grid**: A strict, visible bento-box style grid where every element lives in its own bordered cell.
*   **Asymmetry**: Break the grid occasionally with large, spanning typography or marquee text to create rhythm.
*   **Sticky Elements**: Key data points (like the 2026 election odds) should stick to the viewport edges as the user scrolls.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Marquee Scrollers**: Infinite scrolling text bars showing live ticker updates (e.g., "LULA 45% - BOLSONARO 40%").
2.  **Hard Shadows**: Elements have solid black drop shadows (4px 4px 0px #000) that shift on hover.
3.  **Data Glitch**: Subtle glitch effects on hover over numbers to suggest the flux of real-time data.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Instant Feedback**: Hover states are snappy (0.1s transition). Clicks feel mechanical.
*   **Micro-Interactions**: When a user selects a prediction, the card should "snap" into place or change border color instantly.

**Animation**:
*   **Ticker Tape**: Smooth, continuous linear movement for market updates.
*   **Staggered Reveal**: Grid items load in a rapid, staggered sequence (like a trading terminal booting up).
*   **Hover Jitters**: Slight vibration or displacement on interactive elements to signal "live" energy.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headlines**: **Space Grotesk** or **Chakra Petch** (Bold/Black). Geometric, slightly quirky, feels technical.
*   **Body**: **Inter** or **JetBrains Mono**. Clean, legible, with a coding/terminal vibe for data points.
*   **Hierarchy**: Massive size contrast. Headlines are huge (4rem+), body text is utilitarian (1rem).
</idea>

### <idea>
**Design Movement**: **Glassmorphism & Ethereal Tech**

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Transparency & Depth**: Use layers of frosted glass to suggest depth and complexity without clutter.
2.  **Fluidity**: The market is liquid; the design should feel like a flowing stream of information.
3.  **Luminous Data**: Information should feel like it's glowing or emitting light against a darker backdrop.
4.  **Soft Precision**: Rounded corners and smooth gradients, but with razor-sharp typography for data.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Background**: Deep Navy (#0A0F1C) or Midnight Purple (#120B29) to represent the "unknown future".
*   **Primary**: Cyan to Purple Gradient (Linear) representing the spectrum of possibilities.
*   **Accents**: Neon Mint (#00FF9D) for "Yes/Buy" and Neon Coral (#FF5E5E) for "No/Sell".
*   **Glass**: White with 10-20% opacity and background blur.
*   **Intent**: To make the user feel like they are peering into a crystal ball or a futuristic HUD.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Floating Cards**: Content lives on floating glass panes that drift slightly on scroll (parallax).
*   **Central Axis**: A strong central visual (like a glowing orb or 3D chart) that anchors the hero section, with content orbiting it.
*   **Overlapping Layers**: Elements overlap slightly to emphasize the depth and transparency.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Glowing Orbs**: Abstract, blurred orbs of color moving slowly in the background to represent market sentiment.
2.  **Frosted Borders**: 1px white borders with low opacity to define edges without harsh lines.
3.  **Mesh Gradients**: Backgrounds use complex mesh gradients that shift slowly.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Fluidity**: Hover effects are slow and smooth (0.4s ease-out).
*   **Glow**: Elements glow or brighten when interacted with.
*   **Magnetic**: Buttons have a slight magnetic pull towards the cursor.

**Animation**:
*   **Float**: continuous, subtle floating animation for cards.
*   **Blur In**: Content fades in while unblurring (filter: blur(10px) -> blur(0)).
*   **Gradient Flow**: Text gradients that animate horizontally to draw the eye.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headlines**: **Outfit** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Modern, geometric, friendly but tech-forward.
*   **Body**: **DM Sans**. Highly readable, geometric sans.
*   **Hierarchy**: relied on weight and color (glowing text) rather than just size.
</idea>

### <idea>
**Design Movement**: **Swiss International Style (Digital Remaster)**

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Grid Precision**: Absolute adherence to a mathematical grid. Order is paramount.
2.  **Typography as Image**: Type is the primary visual element. Large, bold, and expressive.
3.  **Objective Clarity**: No decorative fluff. Every element serves a function. "Less is more."
4.  **High Contrast**: Black and white dominance with strategic bursts of signal color.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Background**: Off-White (#F5F5F5) or Paper tone.
*   **Primary**: International Orange (#FF3300) or Cobalt Blue (#0047BB) - classic Swiss colors.
*   **Text**: Stark Black (#000000).
*   **Intent**: To convey authority, reliability, and the "truth" of the data. It feels like a high-end financial newspaper from the future.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Asymmetric Balance**: Content is balanced by negative space, not symmetry.
*   **Typographic Scale**: Massive headlines that span the full width, contrasting with small, dense columns of data.
*   **Horizontal Rules**: Heavy use of horizontal lines to separate sections and data rows.

**Signature Elements**:
1.  **Big Numbers**: Percentages (e.g., "55%") are treated as graphic art, filling entire grid modules.
2.  **Grotesque Type**: Use of a classic Grotesque font with tight letter spacing.
3.  **Geometric Icons**: Simple, solid geometric shapes (circles, squares, triangles) to represent data categories.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Snap**: Interactions are instant and precise. No easing, just state changes.
*   **Inversion**: Hover states often involve full color inversion (black becomes white, white becomes black).

**Animation**:
*   **Slide**: Elements slide in from the bottom or side with a hard stop.
*   **Typewriter**: Data points update with a digital ticker or typewriter effect.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headlines**: **Helvetica Now Display** or **Unica 77** (or open source equivalent like **Inter Tight** or **Switzer**).
*   **Body**: **Roboto Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono** for data tables.
*   **Hierarchy**: Defined by scale and weight. Bold for headers, regular for body.
</idea>
